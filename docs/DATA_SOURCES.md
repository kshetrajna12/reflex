# Data sources for decision models: a verified catalogue

Seventy-five Hugging Face datasets checked on 2026-09-19 for use with reflex's three primitives, grouped by what they teach. Soft-label sources (annotator fractions, human probabilities) are the most valuable for a proper-scoring-rule trainer. NC-licensed sets are marked: evaluation only, never for the MIT-licensed adapter. Sources already used by `reflex-data` are listed in `src/reflex/train/recipes.py`.


Every id below was checked against `https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/<id>` and the datasets-server `splits` / `first-rows` / `size` endpoints. "Script" means the repo carries a loading script named after the repo, which the `datasets` library refuses; where a `refs/convert/parquet` mirror exists it is stated. Sizes are row counts from the datasets-server `size` endpoint. **NC** marks licenses usable for evaluation only (not for the MIT-licensed adapter). Nothing was written into the repository.

Already-used and excluded datasets (banking77, clinc_oos, MMLU-Pro, civil_comments, HaluEval, ms_marco, HelpSteer2, github-codereview, go_emotions, quality, Bitext, toxic-chat, multi_nli, yelp, anything JevBench/Jev-derived) are not re-proposed.

## The five to add first

1. **`ucberkeley-dlab/measuring-hate-speech`.** The richest soft source found: ten ordinal dimensions with roughly three to four raters per comment, CC BY 4.0, large, and it maps directly onto score questions with per-level fractions rather than a single mean.
2. **`Zhengping/UNLI` plus `metaeval/chaos-mnli-ambiguity`.** Direct human probability targets for noul (UNLI, 61k, MIT) and 100-rater distributions for a three-way choice. Together they are the closest thing to calibrated-probability supervision on the hub, though the ChaosNLI SNLI/αNLI parts are missing and the MNLI items need deduping against the external MNLI test.
3. **`nvidia/HelpSteer3` preference.** 40k multi-turn pairwise items with each annotator's −3..3 score preserved, CC BY 4.0, covering code and multilingual chat that HelpSteer2 does not. Good for both choice and score with rater distributions.
4. **`nguha/legalbench` with `coastalcph/lex_glue`.** Fills the long-document rule-application family at two scales. LexGLUE gives volume (scotus, ecthr, case_hold, unfair_tos) under CC BY 4.0. LegalBench gives policy-plus-claim items (insurance, MAUD, contract NLI, SARA tax) with verified per-task licenses; skip the NC tasks listed above for the adapter.
5. **`lockon/xlam-function-calling-60k` with `MadeAgents/xlam-irrelevance-7.5k`, and for SWE `fasterinnerlooper/codereviewer` quality split.** The first pair gives routing over 2–8 named tools with a real no-match class under CC BY 4.0. The CodeReviewer quality split gives 116k hunk-level "needs a review comment" decisions, though its upstream data license is not stated on the mirror.

Next tier: `nvidia/Aegis-AI-Content-Safety-Dataset-1.0` (per-rater safety labels), `YuehHanChen/forecasting_raw` and `nikhilchandak/metaculus-binary` (crowd probabilities and post-cutoff resolved questions), `momererkoc/social_bias_frames` and `Multilingual-Perspectivist-NLU/MultiPICo` (more rater-fraction nouls), `openai/summarize_from_feedback` (multi-worker 7-point axes), `mtc/frank-test-set-with-faithfulness-annotation` (per-rater faithfulness), and `colin/PrimeVul` paired (clean vulnerability pairs).
